Selling your home during a health crisis.

A medical emergency, yours or a loved one's, changes what you need from your home overnight. Here's how to make a sale as easy as possible during an already hard time.

If you're reading this because of a sudden diagnosis, a hospitalization, or a health situation that's changed what you need from your living situation, I'm sorry you're dealing with this on top of everything else. This isn't meant to be a sales pitch during a hard moment, it's meant to lay out your real options clearly, so you can make a decision without added pressure.

Why health situations change the calculus around selling

A health crisis often changes two things at once: your financial picture, with medical costs arriving quickly and sometimes unpredictably, and your physical situation, whether that means needing a single story home, needing to move closer to family or a treatment center, or needing to access home equity to cover care. Any one of these alone would be a lot to manage. Together, they often mean a traditional home sale's typical timeline, and its typical demands on your time and energy, aren't realistic right now.

What a traditional sale asks of you, and why that's often too much right now

Listing a home traditionally means keeping it presentable for showings, coordinating around other people's schedules, potentially making repairs an inspection turns up, and being available for weeks or months of back and forth. During a health crisis, whether you're the one who's sick or you're caring for someone who is, that kind of ongoing demand on your time and attention is often simply not something you have to give right now, and it shouldn't have to be.

Your real options

Ask a family member or trusted friend to manage a traditional listing

If you have someone able to take this on for you, a traditional sale is still on the table, with someone else handling showings and coordination so it doesn't fall on you.

Access a reverse mortgage or home equity line, if staying is the goal

If the goal is to stay in the home but access its equity to cover costs, this is worth discussing with a financial advisor, it's a different path than selling entirely.

Sell directly, on your timeline, with minimal demands on your time

We buy homes as-is, which means no repairs, no staging, no showings to coordinate. We can move quickly if speed matters, or work around your schedule if you need flexibility instead, whatever actually serves your situation.

You're allowed to take your time with this decision

Nothing about reaching out to us commits you to anything. If you want a number to help you think through your options, or just want to understand what a sale could look like without any pressure to act on it, that's a completely reasonable way to use this conversation.

A note on privacy

You're never obligated to explain your medical situation to us in any detail. "I need to sell because of a health situation" is more than enough information for us to work with. We're here to make this one part of a hard time simpler, not to ask you to relive or explain more than you're comfortable sharing.
